How to read this
week = (date − launch) / 7 launch: Korea and Taiwan 2025-11-19, Global 2026-10-05 Korea / Taiwan point = a price written in a forum post or read off an exchange screenshot that day (quoted) or a post that says a price moved without a number (rough, hollow) Korea line = aeid.gg world-market weekly average (api-aeid.lostgld.com /graph-prices, period=week); the Korean refining-stone ladder is named differently from global (장인 = Expert's, 달인 = Artisan's, 달인의 최상급 = Artisan's Ultimate) and its cheap tiers are near worthless there Global point = median over the day of the hourly cheapest ask on aion2.exchange (Nezekan, or the median of NAE Nezekan, Siel, Azphel, Israphel and EU Kaisinel, Azphel) Korean prices are written in 만 (10,000): "사념 250" = 2.5M

Where the Taiwan board posted no number the card says so; Taiwan's posts describe the same arc as Korea (materials "terrifyingly expensive" by their Season 2) without prices. Kina on the three services is not the same currency: Korea's devalued through 2026, the global servers have a 1M-a-day mob cap from launch. Compare shapes and weeks, not absolute Kina. Item icons are served by gamers4.life; items it does not carry show no icon.
